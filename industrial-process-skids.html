Industrial Process Skids

Instron Technologies engineers and integrates modular process skids for defined production and processing duties, combining process engineering, equipment integration, mechanical systems, instrumentation and automation for reliable operation on your site.

Typical applications

Modular process systems for industrial applications

Industrial Process Skids bring related process equipment and supporting systems together in an engineered modular arrangement.

Reaction and mixing

Integrated systems for reaction, blending and mixing duties.

Dosing and formulation

Controlled material addition and formulation processes.

Distillation and solvent recovery

Separation and recovery systems for chemical and industrial applications.

Filtration and separation

Engineered equipment arrangements for fluid treatment and separation duties.

Heating and cooling

Thermal-process systems integrating equipment, utilities and controls.

Gas and fluid handling

Process systems for fluid transfer, pressure management and gas-handling duties.

Integrated system architecture

Five engineering areas, one system

Each area is defined together with the others, so the skid works as one system on site.

Process and equipment engineering

Define operating conditions, equipment functions, process flow and required utilities.

Mechanical and modular design

Develop equipment arrangement, skid layout, piping, access and maintenance requirements.

Instrumentation and automation

Integrate process measurements, control devices, PLC/HMI systems and operating sequences.

Facility and system integration

Define utility connections, equipment interfaces, installation requirements and project responsibilities.

Testing and handover

Set inspection, acceptance, commissioning and documentation requirements.

Equipment classes

Typical integrated equipment classifications

Depending on the process, modular skids may integrate:

  • Reactors and process vessels
  • Filtration and separation equipment
  • Heat-transfer systems
  • Pumps, piping and valves
  • Process instrumentation
  • Electrical and automation systems

Equipment selection depends on the application, operating requirements and agreed engineering scope.

Engineering and acceptance

Buildable and testable deliverables

Our engineering supports coordinated procurement, fabrication, integration and testing through defined technical documentation, grouped in four packages: process; mechanical and skid; electrical, instrumentation and controls; and testing and handover.

See the four packages

Controls and operating interfaces

Controls and data should not be late additions

Automation and monitoring requirements are best set alongside the process-system design. Early coordination defines instrument selection, operating sequences, alarms, interlocks, control-system interfaces and data requirements.

Our Industrial Automation team handles control-system engineering, and PlantON™ by Instron Technologies adds monitoring, trending and reporting where needed.

Scope-specific qualification

Safety and responsibility boundaries

Industrial process skids are engineered around the process hazards, operating conditions, equipment limits and installation requirements. Safety requirements, applicable standards, testing obligations and site responsibilities are set for each application and contract.
