01 / Policy topic

Privacy and information handling

This notice explains how Instron Technologies handles personal information through this website. It is a draft under legal review and will be finalized before launch.

Who is responsible

Instron Technologies Inc. (Toronto, Ontario, Canada) and Instron Technologies LLP (Pune, Maharashtra, India), depending on which office handles your enquiry. A named privacy contact will be listed here.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, work email, organization, country, phone number (optional), project stage and project description, and anything you include in an email or job application. Please do not send confidential process data or sensitive personal details.

What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or maps. If analytics is added later, it will stay off until you switch it on, and you can change your choice at any time.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver engineering projects, consider job applications and keep business records. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Our engineering and sales teams in Canada and India, and service providers that host our website, email and customer records for us. Your information may be handled outside your country, including in Canada and India, with the safeguards the law requires.

How long we keep it and your choices

Only as long as needed, then deleted; retention periods will be published here. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent or object to marketing through Discuss Your Project, and complain to the data protection authority where you live.

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW
  • Name the privacy contact and mailbox
  • Confirm the responsible entity for each region
  • Agree and publish retention periods
  • Confirm hosting, email and CRM providers
  • Decide French, Spanish and Arabic versions

Privacy by region

Your privacy rights by region

The same protections apply to every visitor. Where local law adds something, it is listed below.

PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25

Canada

Ask us for access to or correction of your information at any time. In Quebec, our privacy officer's title and contact will be published here, and any tracking technology stays off unless you switch it on. Complaints: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec.

GDPR

European Union and Netherlands

Rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, answered within one month. Complaints: your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

India

Access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent and raise a grievance with us; a grievance contact will be named here. Further recourse: the Data Protection Board of India.

Personal Data Protection Law

Saudi Arabia

Rights to be informed, to access, correct and destroy your data, and to withdraw consent. Regulator: the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (2025)

Mexico

ARCO rights: access, rectification, cancellation and opposition. A Spanish privacy notice (aviso de privacidad) will be published before launch.

Privacy Act 1988; US state privacy laws

Australia and United States

Where these laws apply to us, you can ask to access or correct your information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. Complaints in Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Draft for legal review. Regional details will be confirmed before launch.

02 / Policy topic

Website terms of use

Website content is general information about engineering capabilities and project approaches. It is not a quotation, engineering certification, regulatory opinion or agreed project deliverable. Final terms covering IP, external links, disclaimers, jurisdiction and contract boundaries require legal review.

TO BE FINALIZED BEFORE LAUNCH
  • Confirm applicable entity and jurisdiction
  • Approve intellectual-property and third-party-content terms
  • Confirm how quotations and contractual documents take precedence

03 / Policy topic

Accessibility

The site design aims to support headings, keyboard access, visible focus, mobile zoom and descriptive media alternatives. That design intent is not a completed accessibility certification; production deployment needs manual assistive-technology and device testing alongside documented accessibility support contact details.

TO BE FINALIZED BEFORE LAUNCH
  • Complete keyboard and screen-reader checks
  • Check zoom, contrast and mobile touch targets
  • Publish accessible feedback route and response owner

04 / Policy topic

Entity and name clarification

Instron Technologies is an industrial engineering and technology business and is an independent company, not affiliated with Instron® (ITW). Corporate contact, warranty and contracting information must identify Instron Technologies Inc. or Instron Technologies LLP as appropriate to the actual engagement.

TO BE FINALIZED BEFORE LAUNCH
  • Verify exact company legal identifiers and contact details
  • Separate partner representation from physical offices
  • Confirm publication and trademark wording
