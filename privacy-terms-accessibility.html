01 / Policy topic

Privacy and information handling

This notice explains how Instron Technologies handles personal information through this website.

Who is responsible

Instron Technologies Inc. (Toronto, Ontario, Canada) for visitors and enquiries from the Americas, Europe and Australia; Instron Technologies LLP (Pune, Maharashtra, India) for India, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. Privacy officer: Rahul Shirude, President, privacy@instrontechnologies.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, work email, organization, country, phone number (optional), project stage and project description, and anything you include in an email or job application. Please do not send confidential process data or sensitive personal details.

What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or maps. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a cookieless tool that counts visits without identifying you and list it here. Anything that could identify or track you would stay off until you switch it on.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver engineering projects, consider job applications and keep business records. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Our engineering and sales teams in Canada and India, and service providers that host our website, email and customer records for us. Your information may be handled outside your country, including in Canada and India, with the safeguards the law requires.

How long we keep it and your choices

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted after 24 months; applications from unsuccessful candidates after 12 months. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent or object to marketing by writing to privacy@instrontechnologies.com or through Discuss Your Project, and complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Privacy by region

Your privacy rights by region

The same protections apply to every visitor. Where local law adds something, it is listed below.

PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25

Canada

Ask us for access to or correction of your information at any time. Privacy officer: Rahul Shirude, President, privacy@instrontechnologies.com. In Quebec, any tracking technology stays off unless you switch it on. A French version of this notice is available on request. Complaints: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec.

GDPR

European Union and Netherlands

Rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, answered within one month. Complaints: your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

India

Access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent and raise a grievance with our grievance officer, Rahul Shirude, at privacy@instrontechnologies.com. Further recourse: the Data Protection Board of India.

Personal Data Protection Law

Saudi Arabia

Rights to be informed, to access, correct and destroy your data, and to withdraw consent. Regulator: the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (2025)

Mexico

ARCO rights: access, rectification, cancellation and opposition. A Spanish privacy notice (aviso de privacidad) is available on request.

Privacy Act 1988; US state privacy laws

Australia and United States

Where these laws apply to us, you can ask to access or correct your information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. Complaints in Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

02 / Policy topic

Website terms of use

Website content is general information about engineering capabilities and project approaches. It is not a quotation, engineering certification, regulatory opinion or agreed project deliverable.

03 / Policy topic

Accessibility

This website is designed to work with clear headings, keyboard access, visible focus, mobile zoom and text alternatives for images. If any part of it is hard to use, tell us through Discuss Your Project and we will help.

04 / Policy topic

Entity and name clarification

Instron Technologies is an industrial engineering and technology business and is an independent company, not affiliated with Instron® (ITW). Corporate contact, warranty and contracting information must identify Instron Technologies Inc. or Instron Technologies LLP as appropriate to the actual engagement.
