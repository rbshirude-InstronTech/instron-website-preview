## Steps
1. **Start a new ChatGPT chat for each file**, so the rules are fresh every time. Use the strongest model available.
2. **Upload the file** (or paste its whole text) and write:
   > Translate this file following the instructions at the top. Give me back the completed .md file with the same structure.
3. **Quick look at what comes back:** it should still have the `[P2-0001]` blocks, with every `FR:` or `ES:` line filled in. If ChatGPT stops partway, write:
   > Continue from [P2-xxxx] in the same format.
4. **Send the returned files back to Claude in this Project** and say "import pack p2".
   - You can send them in batches, for example the industry files first.
   - A page goes live only when all of its files are in.
   - Do not edit the `EN:` lines or the `[P2-…]` IDs.

## If the English changes after you start
These pages were still in review with Sumit and Milind when the pack was made (27 Nov 2026).
- Lines whose English is unchanged are kept.
- Changed lines show in English on the translated page until a small top-up pack (p2b) translates them.
- Nothing needs redoing.

## What Claude does with the files
- **Checks every line:** the codes are intact, numbers and standards match the English, brand and product names are kept, the glossary and menu names are followed, there are no long dashes, and the punctuation suits the language.
- **Loads the lines that pass** and lists any rejected lines with the reason.
- **Reviews and tests:** reads the flagged lines, the headings, claims, figures and the case-study and portfolio facts, then checks the layout on desktop, tablet and phone.
- **Legal check:** Trust & Responsibility will be flagged for the legal review (LEG-01) before going live.
