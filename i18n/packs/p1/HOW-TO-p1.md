# Translation pack p1: how to use it

**What it covers:** the 14 stable pages:
- Solutions
- Pilot & Demonstration Plants

- Applications and its 6 application pages
- Insights and its 3 articles

Only lines not yet translated are included: 611 lines, about 6,400 words per language.

**Files:** 8 files, 4 per language, of 130 to 180 lines each.

## Steps
1. **Start a new ChatGPT chat for each file**, so the rules are fresh every time. Use the strongest model available.
2. **Upload the file** (or paste its whole text) and write:
   > Translate this file following the instructions at the top. Give me back the completed .md file with the same structure.
3. **Quick look at what comes back:** it should still have the `[P1-0001]` blocks, with every `FR:` or `ES:` line filled. If ChatGPT stops partway, write:
   > Continue from [P1-xxxx] in the same format.
4. **Send the returned files back to Claude in this Project** and say "import pack p1".
   - You can send them one at a time or all together.
   - Do not edit the `EN:` lines or the `[P1-…]` IDs.

## What Claude does with them
- **Checks every line:** the codes are intact, numbers and standards match the English, brand and product names are kept, the glossary is followed, there are no long dashes, and the punctuation suits the language.
- **Loads the lines that pass.** A page goes live in that language once all its lines are in.
- **Lists any rejected lines** with the reason, so they can go back to ChatGPT or be fixed by hand.
- **Reads the lines flagged with a warning** and the most visible text (headings, claims, figures, FAQs), then checks the layout on desktop and phone.
