Where are you starting from?

Most projects start from one of three situations. Each needs a different engineering scope.

Technology developers

Your process works in the lab

You are moving a laboratory method or new technology into an instrumented physical system that can support controlled trials, operating data and the next engineering decision.

Pilot & Demonstration Plants

Process plants

You need an industrial skid

You have a defined production or process duty where a compact, skid-mounted or modular system fits, and the operating basis, interfaces and acceptance requirements must be engineered clearly.

Industrial Process Skids

Operating sites

Your plant already runs

You need to modernize PLC/HMI/SCADA or add plant data to installed equipment, where undocumented logic, operator workarounds and tight shutdown windows shape the scope.

Industrial Automation · PlantON™

Before you scale up

The scale-up challenge

A vessel, pump, heater or control panel can meet its own specification and still leave the overall process difficult to operate.

01

Lab results don’t prove scale

Throughput, purity, stability and equipment duty still have to be demonstrated before the next investment decision.

02

Interfaces are left undefined

Equipment, instruments, controls, utilities and site connections are specified separately, not as one system.

03

Acceptance is agreed too late

What must be demonstrated at FAT, SAT or commissioning is not settled before hardware is bought.

04

Pilot learning is lost

Lessons from trials never become the standards, test criteria and procedures the next stage needs.

Define the process basis first. Then make every major system element support it.
