How it works

Connect, contextualize, act

PlantON sits above the control layer. It reads plant data; it never takes control authority.

ConnectPLC and SCADA tags, historians, OEM machines, meters, databases
ContextualizeUnits, timestamps, batch, shift and product context, validated
MonitorLive views, trends and alerts for each role
AnalyzeLosses, consumption and quality against a baseline
ActReports and workflows with an owner for each event

Sources

Existing PLC and SCADA tags, alarms and historian; OEM machines; utility meters and added sensors; SQL, CSV or Excel; ERP and SAP exchange where interfaces permit; manual forms.

Edge

Gateways or industrial PCs where needed, with store-and-forward so a network drop does not leave gaps in the record.

Security & access

Deployed inside your IT/OT policy: defined network zones, customer-approved accounts and remote access, and minimum permissions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does PlantON replace our SCADA, historian or MES?

No. It uses what you already have and adds the reporting, context and traceability layer on top.

Which systems can it connect to?

PLC and SCADA tags, historians, meters, databases, files and ERP interfaces, as agreed for each project.

Can it connect older machines?

Yes, by reading existing controller signals where they can be trusted, or adding meters and gateways, without changing machine control.

Is PlantON a software licence we set up ourselves?

No. It is delivered with engineering, onboarding, configuration, validation and support.

Can we see it remotely?

Yes, through browser or mobile views where your IT/OT policy allows, with customer-approved accounts and remote-access methods.

How is ROI measured?

Against a baseline agreed before deployment, and reported with its measurement period.
