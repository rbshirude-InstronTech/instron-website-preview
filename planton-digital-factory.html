PlantON by Instron Technologies · Industrial Data & Visibility

Make plant data useful for the decisions your team makes every day.

Plants generate information across PLCs, SCADA, machines, instruments, recipes, utility meters, quality records and production systems. The challenge is giving that information an operating context people can rely on.

At one dairy, PlantON cut compliance reporting time by 94%, with 12% less chemical and 11% less utility use.

PlantON is available on its own for existing plants, and can be included in the process skid plants we deliver where operating data matters from day one.

01 / Understanding the requirement

Start with the decision, not the dashboard

PlantON by Instron Technologies can organize selected plant data into operating views, reporting workflows and traceability records. PlantON complements the PLC/HMI/SCADA control layer; it does not replace source instruments, validated control logic or the plant team that owns the process.
