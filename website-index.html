Energy Transition & Critical Minerals

Cleantech and critical-material technologies often begin with a strong scientific or process result. The next stage introduces new questions around operating duration, recycle behavior, heat and mass transfer, maintainability, controls, utilities, safety, site interfaces and the quality of the data behind the next decision.

Explore

Research & Technology Development

A principal investigator may focus on the scientific variable, while facilities teams think about utilities and maintainability. EHS considers operating boundaries, procurement needs a clear scope, and commercialization leaders need evidence that supports the next decision.

Explore

Chemicals, Refining & Consumer Products

Specialty and fine-chemical processes rarely fail because one pump, one valve or one PLC tag is missing. The harder problems sit at the interfaces: batch routes, recovery loops, feed variation, process control, operator workflow and installed plant constraints.

Explore

Food, Beverage & Dairy

Food and beverage operations can run on reliable controls yet still struggle to connect batch history, cleaning performance, line stops and utilities with the decisions people need to make. The engineering task starts by understanding the process and the records already available.

Explore

Pharma & Life Sciences

Recipes, setpoint changes, operator actions, alarms, equipment states and supporting records may sit in different systems. Controls engineering and record design therefore need a shared technical basis, with quality and validation responsibility assigned explicitly.

Explore

Industrial Manufacturing

Mixed-vintage machines, weak downtime data and traceability that stops at the machine boundary: we define the operating truth at machine and line level first, then the controls and data to support it.

Applications

Technology Scale-Up & Commercialization

A working experiment does not settle the questions behind demonstration or commercial deployment. The next system must account for equipment, utilities, instrumentation, operating ranges, maintainability, safety and the evidence needed to make the next investment decision.

Explore

Separation & Solvent Recovery

Recovery and product purity matter, but so do feed variation, utilities, solvent management, fouling, operator actions and safe operation. An effective separation system is more than a column, vessel or membrane package.

Explore

Brownfield Automation Modernization

Replacing an old PLC or SCADA platform is only one part of modernization. The installed plant may contain undocumented logic, operator workarounds, mixed equipment, aging instruments and tight shutdown windows. A responsible upgrade starts with that operating reality.

Explore

Batch & Process Automation

A batch may involve manual decisions, equipment-local controls, PLC sequences, recipes, material movements and data records. The automation scope should bring those steps together without hiding what the operators actually need to see and do.

Explore

Production Visibility & OEE

Production teams can disagree on performance even when a dashboard reports a single percentage. The definitions of running time, planned stops, counts, scrap, speed and downtime reasons determine whether the result supports a useful decision.

Explore

Utility & Energy Monitoring

A monthly total can show that a resource increased without identifying the line, batch, area, asset or condition that drove it. Useful utility monitoring begins with measurement boundaries, reliable sources and the production context needed to investigate change.
