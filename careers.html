Our working culture

Curiosity and ownership, grounded in engineering evidence.

Learning is most useful when it connects to a real technical decision.

Ask the right question.

Understand the process and intended use before choosing equipment or software.

Own the handoff.

Document what is complete, what remains unresolved and who makes the next decision.

Learn from testing.

Drawings, acceptance records and site findings should improve the next project.

Disciplines

The work is multidisciplinary, but ownership stays clear.

Process & Scale-Up Engineering

P&IDs, equipment and instrument definition, process integration, operating envelopes and test documentation.

Industrial Automation & Controls

PLC/HMI/SCADA design, I/O, panels, commissioning and problem-solving around working industrial systems.

PlantON™ & Industrial Data

Production context, measurement, reporting, traceability and data integration with source-system discipline.

Project Engineering

Scope, procurement interfaces, test preparation, quality documentation, stakeholder alignment and handover.

  • Your preferred engineering discipline or role

  • Technical skills or projects (public or sanitized examples only)
  • Preferred work location
