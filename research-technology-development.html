Facilities and EHS involved late

Rigs that cannot be modified

Every new campaign needs a rebuild instead of a change.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with universities and research institutes?

Yes. Research programs are a large part of our pilot-system work, including 8+ research projects in Saudi Arabia since 2024.

Can you automate the reactors we already have?

Yes. We retain existing reactors and add pH control, dosing, temperature, agitation and PLC/SCADA.

How is a research system accepted?

Against the test objective, with FAT and SAT checks and a data handover agreed at the start.

Can the system be changed for later campaigns?

Yes. We design for modification and future scale where the program needs it.
