Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver 21 CFR Part 11 compliant systems?

Yes. We deliver 21 CFR Part 11 compliant control systems with audit trails, electronic records and user access control.

Do you execute IQ and OQ?

Yes. We execute IQ and OQ with your QA team. Your quality authority approves the system for use.

Which approach do you follow for validation documentation?

GAMP 5. We produce the URS support, FDS, test protocols and reports for your validation package.

Can you work in hazardous areas?

Yes. Our API skid projects include hazardous-area field instruments and unmanned operation.

Do you provide the control panels?

Yes. We provide control panels with UL 508A, CSA or CE approval, whichever your market needs.
