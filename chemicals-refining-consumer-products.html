Common challenges

Where chemical and consumer-products plants typically lose output

Each of these costs solvent, capacity or product quality.

Solvent going to waste

Recovery that misses purity or throughput sends solvent back to waste and the plant keeps buying fresh.

Batches that do not repeat

Manual dosing, heating and operator timing change the product from shift to shift.

Capacity capped by manual work

Fixed-speed equipment and hand-run batching limit output and changeovers.

Upgrades around a running plant

Old vessels, panels and networks that must keep producing during the change.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you design a recovery system for our stream?

Yes. We start from your feed and product specifications and design the skid, column and controls together.

Can you upgrade controls without a long shutdown?

Yes. We plan phased cutovers with rollback paths and test high-risk changes at FAT.

Which control platforms do you work with?

Rockwell, Siemens, Schneider and Honeywell. Platform choice follows your installed base.

Do you test skids before shipping?

Where the system allows it. FAT and SAT scope is agreed per project; some systems are fully tested in the shop, others are verified on site.
