Our story

One project basis across several engineering disciplines.

Our work connects the disciplines needed to turn a concept or operating requirement into an engineered system.

A pilot project may need process, mechanical, instrumentation, automation and data-acquisition decisions. An industrial modernization may need controls, installed-equipment review and site commissioning. We make those boundaries explicit instead of leaving them to be discovered during execution.

How we think

Credibility comes from how the work is defined.

01

Process basis before fabrication.

Define the objective, operating envelope, engineering assumptions and review responsibility before major purchases or implementation commitments.

02

One responsibility map.

Separate Instron Technologies, customer, suppliers and local specialist responsibilities so cross-discipline gaps are visible and managed.

03

Evidence matched to stage.

A research pilot, an industrial modernization and a production system each need acceptance criteria appropriate to their stage and intended use.
